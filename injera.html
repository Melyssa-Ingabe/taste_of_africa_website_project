Injera

Region: East Africa • Country: Ethiopia • Fermentation time: 3 days

Injera is the soft, spongy, slightly sour flatbread at the centre of every Ethiopian and Eritrean meal. It is used as both the plate and the spoon: stews are served on top and torn pieces of injera are used to scoop them up. It is traditionally made from teff, a tiny gluten-free grain native to Ethiopia and Eritrea, which is fermented with a starter called ersho to give injera its tangy flavour and bubbly texture. This page shows how to make the starter, which is the first and most important step.

Ingredients

For the starter

  • 2 cups white or red teff flour
  • 1.5 litres lukewarm water
  • 500 ml cold water, for storing the starter in the fridge

Alternative flours (if you can't find teff)

  • Wheat flour (all-purpose)
  • Barley flour
  • Rice flour
  • Millet flour
  • Quinoa flour

Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Mix the flour and water

Put the teff flour in a large bowl or container. Slowly add the lukewarm water while mixing until the flour and water are fully combined.

Step 2: Ferment for three days

Cover the container and leave it at room temperature, for example on the kitchen counter, for three days. This is when the natural fermentation happens.

Step 3: Drain the liquid

After three days you should see air bubbles on top, and a layer of liquid will have separated. Pour this top liquid away. The thick starter underneath is your ersho.

Step 4: Use or store the starter

You can use all of the starter right away. To keep it for later, add about 500 ml of cold water, cover, and store it in the fridge. It can be kept for up to three months, but remember to change the water at least once a week.

Next step: Cooking the injera (general method)

This part is not shown in the video. In general, the fermented batter is thinned with water to a pourable consistency, poured in a spiral onto a hot non-stick pan or traditional clay griddle (mitad), covered, and cooked on one side only until bubbles appear and the edges lift. Injera is not flipped.
